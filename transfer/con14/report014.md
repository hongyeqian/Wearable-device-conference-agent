Discussion on Internship and Course Registration Policies

# High-Level Summary

The meeting focused on discussing the policies surrounding internships and course registration for students. Key topics included the guidelines for internships, course registration requirements, leave of absence policies, project development, and the status of credit-bearing courses.

---

## 1️. Internship Opportunities

Brief Summary:

- The discussion highlighted that students can undertake full-time internships as long as they do not exceed 16 hours per week. It was clarified that unpaid internships do not trigger work pass requirements, and overseas internships are prohibited during term time.

Key Takeaways:

- Students can do full-time internships without exceeding 16 hours.
- Unpaid internships do not require work passes.
- Overseas internships are not allowed during term time.

---

## 2. Course Registration and Requirements

Brief Summary:

- The conversation covered the course registration process, emphasizing that students can take courses 6222 in T3 and 6211 in T1, but must pay for additional academic units (AUs). Approval from the school is required for any changes in course registration.

Key Takeaways:

- Students must pay for additional AUs when taking multiple courses.
- Approval from the school is necessary for course registration changes.
- A self-source project for 6211 must be submitted by December 19.

---

## 3️. Leave of Absence (LOA) Policies

Brief Summary:

- It was discussed that students applying for a leave of absence must provide a clear plan for their absence, and official documentation is required for health or family-related LOA applications. Students cannot remain in Singapore if their student pass is canceled due to LOA.

Key Takeaways:

- A clear plan is required for LOA applications.
- Official documentation is needed for health or family-related LOAs.
- Students cannot stay in Singapore if their student pass is canceled due to LOA.

---

## 4. Project Development and Supervision

Brief Summary:

- The meeting addressed the guidelines for project development, stating that students can expand upon previous projects for 6211 as long as they are distinct. Approval and a clear description are required for self-source projects, and students should confirm project details with supervisors before deadlines.

Key Takeaways:

- Students can expand on previous projects for 6211, ensuring they are distinct.
- Self-source projects require approval and a clear description.
- Confirmation from supervisors is needed before deadlines.

## 5. Credit Bearing Courses

Brief Summary:

- The discussion confirmed that courses 6222, 6223, and 6211 are credit-bearing. Documentation is available to prove course contributions towards graduation, and students must follow formal processes for these courses.

Key Takeaways:

- Courses 6222, 6223, and 6211 are all credit-bearing.
- Documentation is available to support course contributions to graduation.
- Students must adhere to formal processes for credit-bearing courses.
