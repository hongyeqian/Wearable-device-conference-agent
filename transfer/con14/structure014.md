Discussion on Internship and Course Registration Policies

- Internship Opportunities
  - Students can undertake full-time internships as long as they do not exceed 16 hours.
  - Internships can be unpaid without triggering work pass requirements.
  - Overseas internships are not allowed during term time.

- Course Registration and Requirements
  - Students can take 6222 in T3 and 6211 in T1, but must pay for additional AU credits.
  - Approval from the school is required for course registration changes.
  - Students need to submit a self-source project for 6211 by December 19.

- Leave of Absence (LOA) Policies
  - Students applying for LOA must provide a clear plan for their absence.
  - Official documentation is required for health or family-related LOA applications.
  - Students cannot stay in Singapore if their student pass is canceled due to LOA.

- Project Development and Supervision
  - Students can expand upon previous projects for 6211, but must ensure they are distinct.
  - Self-source projects require approval and a clear description.
  - Students can discuss potential projects with supervisors, but confirmation is needed before deadlines.

- Credit Bearing Courses
  - Courses 6222, 6223, and 6211 are all credit bearing.
  - Documentation is available to prove course contributions to graduation.
  - Students must follow formal processes for credit-bearing courses.
